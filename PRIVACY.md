## Seamloop Privacy Policy

Updated: August 5, 2026

Seamloop does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

It makes no network requests of its own, contains no analytics/telemetry, and shares nothing with anyone.

Seamloop processes the audio of the current YouTube/YouTube Music video entirely on your device.

This audio, used to detect where music repeats, is held only in memory and discarded upon leaving the page.

Seamloop runs only on `youtube.com`/`music.youtube.com` and requests permission for `storage`.

Your extension settings are saved locally in browser storage and never leave your device.

If this ever changes, this policy and store listings will be updated first.
